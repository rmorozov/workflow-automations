# CP-007 Calibrate capacity rates from ADO history

Design: [Team capacity model design](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/ff3e10f9-39d4-4a50-935e-c54f795d2ea5).

The bug-fix quota (50%) and focus loss (12%) take 62% of the base and are guesses:
±10 points of bug quota moves net capacity by ±26%, while the whole calendar moves
it by a few percent. Measure them instead. From a saved ADO snapshot of closed and
active work in past iterations (no live ADO access needed), compute per AreaPath and
iteration: completed hours on Bugs, completed hours on other work, and the
model's predicted net hours for the same iteration. Write a calibration sheet with
the observed bug share, the ratio of completed change-request hours to predicted
net hours (estimate accuracy), and suggested `rates` rows per AreaPath and phase
that can be pasted into the config. Nothing changes the config automatically.

Before relying on it, agree with the teams what CR estimates contain: if they
already include code review, testing or bug fixing, the model deducts them twice.
Record the rule in the specification.

## Acceptance evidence

A synthetic snapshot of three past iterations for two teams yields the expected bug
shares and accuracy ratios; iterations without logged work are reported, not
counted as zero; the suggested rows load as a valid `rates` sheet.
