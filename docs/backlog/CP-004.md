# CP-004 team-roster lists unmapped and email-less people

Design: [Team capacity model design](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/ff3e10f9-39d4-4a50-935e-c54f795d2ea5).

`dev_per_area` silently leaves out approved people with no AreaPath, and counts
emails, so a person without one shows as zero. Add a `problems` sheet listing each
approved person with no AreaPath, no email or an unclassified title, with the
reason, so gaps are fixed in the mapping before a capacity run.

## Acceptance evidence

A synthetic roster with one person of each kind lists exactly those three people
with their reasons; the other sheets are unchanged.
