# CP-010 Calibration evidence columns and parent/child deduplication

From the [model review on PR #48](https://github.com/rmorozov/workflow-automations/pull/48#issuecomment-6292432126), narrowed in the thread (Ruslan, 2026-10-20).

Calibration rows show a ratio and a suggestion, but not how much evidence is
behind them. Add per row: the number of items and people with logged work, the
share of the pool's predicted base that was logged (coverage), and the excluded
hours by reason. Mark rows with a `low evidence` flag below a configurable
threshold; show the flag and keep the suggestion, so people decide (no hard
suppression). Count CompletedWork once when a parent and its children both carry
it (children win). Document current-assignee attribution as a known limit.

## Acceptance evidence

Tests: evidence columns for two teams; a parent and child both logging hours are
counted once; a row below the threshold is flagged and still listed; two
iterations with identical totals but different coverage are told apart by the
coverage column.
