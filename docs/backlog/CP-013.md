# CP-013 Scenario sensitivity and a stated delivery claim

From the [model review on PR #48](https://github.com/rmorozov/workflow-automations/pull/48#issuecomment-6292432126). Settle the issue; a full forecasting system is not in scope.

±10 points of bug-fix quota moves net hours by about ±26% at the defaults, yet the
output is one precise value. Developer hours also do not imply testing,
architecture, integration or acceptance capacity, nor that work is ready and
unblocked. Add low, base and high scenarios with visible assumptions for the
dominant rates and estimate uncertainty, without calling them confidence intervals
before validation, and keep one clearly selected scenario in the ado-triage
contract. State prominently that the result estimates developer-effort
availability, not delivery feasibility. Full dependency scheduling and skill
constraints stay out of scope.

## Acceptance evidence

A scenarios sheet with low, base and high values and their assumptions;
`dev_per_area_capacity` uses the selected scenario and is unchanged at base; a
synthetic case where developer capacity is sufficient but another role or a
dependency prevents delivery is documented in the spec and README.
