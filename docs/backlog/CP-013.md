# CP-013 Sensitivity of net hours to the dominant rates

From the [model review on PR #48](https://github.com/rmorozov/workflow-automations/pull/48#issuecomment-6292432126), narrowed in the thread (Ruslan, 2026-10-20).

One precise net value hides that ±10 points of bug-fix quota move it by about ±26%.
Add a `sensitivity` sheet per AreaPath and month: net hours of the triage pool at the
configured rates and with bug-fix quota ±10 points and focus loss ±5 points (steps
configurable). `dev_per_area_capacity` keeps the configured rates; ado-triage takes
one number. No scenario engine or confidence intervals. The spec's limits section
already states that the output is developer-effort availability, not delivery
feasibility.

## Acceptance evidence

Tests: the sensitivity rows at zero steps equal `dev_per_area_capacity`; each step
moves net hours as the formula predicts; rates are clamped so a step never makes
quota plus focus exceed 1.
