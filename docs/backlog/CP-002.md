# CP-002 Role groups and capacity taxes

Design: [Team capacity model design](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/ff3e10f9-39d4-4a50-935e-c54f795d2ea5).

Turn available hours into net hours per person and day:

H = (h - daily rituals - iteration rituals / available days in iteration) x (1 - unbooked sick share) x (1 - unbooked vacation share) x (1 - bug-fix quota - focus loss) x allocation x ramp-up

(both shares come from CP-001)

`role_groups` maps each `classified_title` to a group with a default allocation and
flags for rituals, bug-fix quota and triage pool; a roster `allocation` column
overrides the default. `rituals` lists per-day and per-iteration items with hours
and groups (defaults: daily scrum 0.5 h and code review 1 h per day; pre-planning,
demo and retro 1 h and 1 on 1 0.5 h per iteration). Defaults: bug-fix quota 50% for
developer groups and focus loss 12%.

Rates change by team and by phase (more bug fixing in release stabilization, less
in active development or behind a good quality gate). A `rates` sheet sets them:
AreaPath, from, to, optional phase label, bug-fix quota, focus loss. A row applies
to its AreaPath and every AreaPath below it, inside its dates; the deepest
AreaPath wins, then the latest-starting range; a blank cell inherits from the next
matching row, then the default. Overlapping rows at the same depth with the same
start are an error.

Ramp-up: a joiner works at a reduced share after `start_date` (default 50% for the
first 3 months, set in `settings`, overridable per person), so new hires do not
count at full speed from day one.

A person with no group, no AreaPath or no email goes to the `problems` list.

## Acceptance evidence

The worked example (one developer, 10 days, a 4% sick share, no vacation left) gives 80 gross, 18.5
ritual, 2.46 illness, 29.52 bug-fix, 7.08 focus and 22.44 net hours. A person
absent for a whole iteration pays no rituals for it; allocation 0.5 halves every
component; an unmapped title class is reported, not dropped; a `rates` row for
`Proj\Dept` with a stabilization range changes only that subtree in those dates,
and a deeper row overrides it; a joiner's first three months count at 50%.
