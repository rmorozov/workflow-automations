# CP-014 Demand quality beside capacity in triage

From the [model review on PR #48](https://github.com/rmorozov/workflow-automations/pull/48#issuecomment-6292432126), narrowed in the thread (Ruslan, 2026-10-20).

Precise capacity is wasted when the demand it is compared with is unreliable.
ado-triage already counts estimation debt and stale items per team, but Load_Pct
and RAG read as equally certain either way. Next to Load_Pct, show the share of
scope (items and hours) that has no hours estimate or is stale, and mark a team's
RAG as low confidence when that share passes a configurable threshold. Serial with
the other tasks that edit ado_triage.py.

## Acceptance evidence

A synthetic snapshot where one team has 40% unestimated scope shows the share and
a low-confidence mark beside an unchanged Load_Pct; a team with full estimates shows
none; the threshold option changes the mark.
