# CP-011 Calibrate on work closed inside the iteration and report closure mismatches

From the [model review on PR #48](https://github.com/rmorozov/workflow-automations/pull/48#issuecomment-6292432126), narrowed in the thread (Ruslan, 2026-10-20), and corrected in his re-review.

Cumulative CompletedWork counted in an item's current iteration misplaces effort
when an item moves. A snapshot cannot show moves: an item that logged 30 hours in
S1, moved to S2, logged 10 more and closed inside S2 looks like 40 hours of S2 work,
and an item can close after its iteration ends without ever moving. So this task is
a closure/iteration mismatch diagnostic only, and movement stays unknown:

- optionally count only items whose `Microsoft.VSTS.Common.ClosedDate` falls inside
  their iteration's dates;
- report how many completed items, and their hours, closed outside their iteration,
  labelled as closure mismatches, never as moves.

Decision rule: the mismatch count cannot show that moves are rare. Whether to build
revision-based history (a new ADO fetch in ado-triage or ado-graph) is decided by a
small sampled history audit, a separate option, not by this count. A synthetic
backtest alone proves the harness, not the model.

## Acceptance evidence

Tests: with the option, an item closed after its iteration's end is excluded and
counted as a closure mismatch; the moved-then-closed-in-S2 item passes the filter,
keeps all 40 hours in S2 and is not reported; without the option, results are
unchanged. The output and spec never call the figure a measure of moves, and the
spec records that movement remains unknown and the decision rule above.
