# CP-009 Separate availability, allocation and productivity

From the [model review on PR #48](https://github.com/rmorozov/workflow-automations/pull/48#issuecomment-6292432126). Settle the issue; a full forecasting system is not in scope.

Today every component, including gross hours, absence and rituals, is multiplied by
allocation x ramp-up, and FTE includes ramp-up. A newcomer at half productivity still
sits through a one-hour meeting for one hour, and mentoring takes an experienced
person's time. A person split 50/50 across two teams may attend both teams'
ceremonies rather than half of each. Keep actual scheduled and absence hours, and
distinguish contractual FTE, allocated FTE and effective estimated-work capacity.
Document the order and meaning of deductions, and decide whether code review is a
fixed overhead or workload-linked effort. An explicit manual mentoring commitment
(hours per period on the mentor) is enough at first; no onboarding simulator.

## Acceptance evidence

Tests for a half-time employee, a 50/50 split with each team's own ceremonies, and a
newcomer with a named mentor commitment show unscaled scheduled, absence and ritual
hours, the three FTE measures, and net hours that change only through the documented
deductions.
