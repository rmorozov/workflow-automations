# CP-009 Ramp-up scales productive time only; split-team ceremonies and mentoring

From the [model review on PR #48](https://github.com/rmorozov/workflow-automations/pull/48#issuecomment-6292432126), narrowed in the thread (Ruslan, 2026-10-20).

Today allocation x ramp-up multiplies every component, so a newcomer at half speed
pays half of each meeting. Apply allocation to the person's time and ramp-up only to
the productive remainder after rituals, so rituals and leave stay at their real
hours. Let a person split across AreaPaths attend each team's ceremonies in full
(a per-row flag or ritual setting) instead of a share of each. Add an explicit
mentoring commitment: hours per iteration charged to a named mentor while a joiner
ramps up. Report FTE without ramp-up; ramp-up shows as its own hour component.
Whether code review is fixed overhead or workload-linked follows CP-008's contract.
No separate contractual, allocated and effective FTE measures.

## Acceptance evidence

Tests: a newcomer at 50% ramp-up pays full ritual hours and half the productive
hours; a 50/50 split person with separate ceremonies pays both teams' rituals; a
mentor commitment lowers the mentor's net by exactly its hours; FTE no longer
changes with ramp-up; components still add up to gross.
