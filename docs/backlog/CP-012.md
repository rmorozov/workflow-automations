# CP-012 Known leave balances and disclosed leave assumptions

From the [model review on PR #48](https://github.com/rmorozov/workflow-automations/pull/48#issuecomment-6292432126). Settle the issue; a full forecasting system is not in scope.

Prorating from `absences_from` assumes leave before that date was taken
proportionally; it does not show that it was. Someone saving most leave for
December gets overstated November and December availability, and even spreading
keeps the annual total while missing monthly peaks. Support an optional remaining
balance per person as of a date, with clear precedence over historical and future
bookings and no double counting. Distinguish booked, balance-backed and assumed
leave in the outputs. Decide how approved leave above the expected budget and
carry-over are represented. Monthly distribution weights can come later.

## Acceptance evidence

Tests for saved year-end leave, a joiner and a leaver with balances, a window
crossing New Year and changing forecast start dates show no double counting;
`capacity_by_person` splits leave into booked, balance-backed and assumed hours.
