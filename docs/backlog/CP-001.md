# CP-001 Working-day calendar for the capacity model

Design: [Team capacity model design](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/ff3e10f9-39d4-4a50-935e-c54f795d2ea5).

Start `team-capacity` with the calendar: read the config workbook (`settings`,
`holidays`, `absences`, optional `iterations`) and the roster's person rows, and
compute each person's available hours per day. Working days are Monday to Friday
minus holidays and the person's absences (whole or part days), within optional
`start_date`/`end_date`, at `hours_per_day` (default from `settings`, 8). An
iteration is 10 working days on a fixed cadence from `iteration_start`; holidays
reduce its hours and never stretch it. An `iterations` sheet replaces the cadence.
Every input error (unknown email in `absences`, overlapping iterations, bad dates)
fails before any output is written.

## Acceptance evidence

Synthetic people and calendars show: holidays and absences remove exactly their
days, part days remove their hours, joiners and leavers count only inside their
dates, an iteration crossing a month border splits its days by month, and every
validation failure exits non-zero with the offending row named.
