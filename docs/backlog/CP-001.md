# CP-001 Working-day calendar for the capacity model

Design: [Team capacity model design](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/ff3e10f9-39d4-4a50-935e-c54f795d2ea5).

Start `team-capacity` with the calendar: read the config workbook (`settings`,
`calendar`, `absences`, optional `iterations`) and the roster's person rows, and
compute each person's available hours per day. Each country has a default week in
`settings` (Monday to Friday, 8 h); the `calendar` sheet lists exception days per
country (date, hours: 0 holiday, 7 short day, 8 a working Saturday). A person's
country comes from a roster `country` column, else a per-AreaPath default. The
`absences` sheet (email, from, to, `type` vacation/sick/other, optional hours)
removes taken or booked days exactly. Two annual budgets in working days cover days
not booked yet, each per country in `settings` and overridable per person
(`vacation_days`, `sick_days`): the vacation entitlement, and the sick days people
are expected to take (not the legal ceiling). Defaults: 7 working days of sick
leave and 30 calendar days of paid leave per year; a calendar-day entitlement and
its bookings are counted in calendar days, then converted at the country's
working-day ratio (30 x 5/7 = 21.4 working days). For each budget, the year's days
minus that type's booked days are spread as an expected share over the person's
unbooked working days from the window start to year end, each calendar year
separately; an over-booked person gets 0 for that budget and a `problems` entry.
Optional `start_date`/`end_date` and `hours_per_day`
cover joiners, leavers and part-time. An iteration is two whole weeks, Monday to the
next Friday, on a fixed cadence from `iteration_start`; holidays reduce its hours and
never move its end. An `iterations` sheet replaces the cadence. Work is per day and
summed into iterations and months, because weekly totals cannot be split correctly
between months. Every input error (unknown email or country, overlapping
iterations, an iteration not starting on Monday, bad dates) fails before any output
is written.

## Acceptance evidence

Synthetic people and calendars show: holidays and absences remove exactly their
days, a short day lowers hours but keeps the day, a working Saturday adds one,
part days remove their hours, the week of Dec 29 with Jan 1 and 2 off puts all its
hours in December, 30 unbooked calendar days of leave become 21.4 working days
spread over the rest of the year, booked sick days reduce only the sick budget, a booking later in
the year lowers the spread share without counting leave twice, joiners and leavers count only inside their
dates, an iteration crossing a month border splits its days by month, and every
validation failure exits non-zero with the offending row named.
